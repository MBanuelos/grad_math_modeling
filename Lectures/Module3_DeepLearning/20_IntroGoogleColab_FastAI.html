37.2 FastAI

  • FastAI is a library that simplifies training neural networks
  • It has models that are predefined for a variety of tasks
  • Started by Jeremy Howard and Rachel Thomas

37.3 Google Colab

  • Colab is a Python development environment that runs in the browser using Google Cloud.
  • Works very similar to running Jupyter Notebooks on your machine.
  • You can start by navigating to https://colab.research.google.com/
